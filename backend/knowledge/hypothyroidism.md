# Hypothyroidism

> Demo reference summary authored for this application. Verify against current guidelines (e.g. ATA).

## Clinical features

Fatigue, cold intolerance, weight gain, constipation, dry skin, hair thinning, menstrual irregularity, depression, cognitive slowing, bradycardia and delayed relaxation of reflexes. Hypothyroidism is a reversible cause of fatigue, hyperlipidaemia and macrocytosis.

## Diagnosis

Elevated TSH with low free T4 indicates primary hypothyroidism; elevated TSH with normal free T4 is subclinical hypothyroidism. The most common cause in iodine-sufficient regions is autoimmune (Hashimoto) thyroiditis with positive anti-TPO antibodies.

## Treatment

Levothyroxine, taken on an empty stomach 30–60 minutes before breakfast and separated from iron, calcium and proton pump inhibitors, which reduce absorption. Start lower doses in older adults or those with coronary disease. Recheck TSH 6–8 weeks after starting or changing the dose; target TSH within the reference range.
