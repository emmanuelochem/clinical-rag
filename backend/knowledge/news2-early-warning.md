# NEWS2: National Early Warning Score

> Demo reference summary authored for this application. Source: Royal College of Physicians, NEWS2 (2017).

## Parameters

NEWS2 scores six parameters: respiratory rate, oxygen saturation (scale 1, or scale 2 for confirmed hypercapnic respiratory failure), supplemental oxygen use, systolic blood pressure, pulse rate, level of consciousness or new confusion (ACVPU), and temperature. Each scores 0–3; the aggregate ranges from 0 to 20.

## Thresholds and response

- Aggregate 0–4: low clinical risk; ward-based response.
- A score of 3 in any single parameter: low–medium risk; urgent ward-based response with clinician review.
- Aggregate 5–6: medium risk; urgent response threshold — urgent review by a clinician competent in assessing acutely ill patients.
- Aggregate 7 or more: high risk; emergency response — continuous monitoring and emergency assessment by a team with critical care competencies.

## Notes

A NEWS2 of 5 or more should prompt consideration of sepsis in patients with known or suspected infection. NEWS2 is not validated in children or pregnancy.
